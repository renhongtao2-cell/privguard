## Detailed Description
```
PrivGuard removes image watermarks and logos without cropping or changing the composition — entirely inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Why PrivGuard:
- 100% local: no upload endpoint, no server, no third-party GPU
- Watermark remover: brush over the mark, inpaint on-device (OpenCV.js)
- Background remover: cut out people/products with an on-device AI model
- Built for legal, healthcare, finance & HR teams that cannot send files to a third party
- Free, no account required

How to use:
1. Click the PrivGuard icon
2. Drop or pick an image
3. Brush the watermark area
4. Click Remove — done, on your device

No data leaves your machine. Ever.
```
