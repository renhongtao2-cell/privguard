PrivGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-24

1. No uploads, by design

PrivGuard processes every image and video entirely inside your own browser. There is no server-side upload endpoint — your files are read from your device, processed on your device, and the output is written back to your device. We never receive, store, or transmit your media.

2. No account required

The core tools run without any login. We do not collect personal identifiers to use the local processing features.

3. Analytics

If you use the hosted web app, we may collect anonymized, aggregated usage analytics (page views, feature usage) that cannot be tied back to your files. No file content is ever included.

4. Third-party models

Open-source engines (OpenCV.js, @imgly/background-removal, ffmpeg.wasm — all MIT) are loaded from public CDNs on first use. Only the code library is downloaded; your files are not sent to those CDNs.
