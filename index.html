FAQ

Questions your DPO will ask

Do you store my files?

No. There is no server-side upload path. Files are read from the device, processed in the browser, and the output is written back to the device.

Where does the AI model run?

Entirely on the user's machine via WebAssembly / ONNX. The model file is downloaded once; inference never leaves the device.

Can we self-host?

Yes. The app is a static build — drop it on your own VPS, intranet, or air-gapped network. Team & Enterprise plans include the self-host license.

Is this GDPR / EU AI Act aligned?

Because no personal data is transferred to us, the main Art. 28 / Art. 44 risks are architecturally removed. We provide a DPA template and audit-log feature for Enterprise.
